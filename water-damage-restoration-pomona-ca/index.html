Water on the floor in Pomona, right now

Find where it is coming from. A burst pipe, a failed hose and a backed-up drain need different first moves, and the wrong one wastes minutes. Our dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and the first question we ask is what the water looks like.

Clear water from a supply line is the easiest case. Gray water from an appliance drain or a rise from a floor drain is not, and sewage is a health hazard that calls for flood damage restoration methods and protective gear. Tell the dispatcher which one you have.

If water is still spreading

These five steps take a few minutes and can save a lot of drywall.

  • Main valve, clockwiseClose it completely. If you rent, ask the landlord or manager for the shutoff location today so you know it before an emergency.
  • Electricity with careTurn off breakers to wet rooms only if you can reach the panel dry. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay away and call the utility or 911.
  • Furniture and valuables upLift electronics, files, shoes and bags onto dry shelving. Put foil or plastic under furniture legs on wet carpet.
  • Contaminated means keep outDo not let children or pets near water from toilets, drains or the outside. Wash hands well after any contact.
  • Dial the 24/7 lineGive the street address, the source and how many rooms are wet. Photos taken before cleanup help your insurance claim.

What the crew does once it arrives

Drying is a process, not a single visit. This is the typical order, and timing varies with materials and conditions.

StageWhat happensEquipment
AssessFind the source, sort wet from dry, set the water categoryMoisture meter, thermal camera

Pomona's water and sewer systems

The city's Water Resources Department runs the water system, and its page says over three-quarters of supply is groundwater. For no-water or street leaks, call the department. A leak inside your walls is yours to fix and to dry.

Pomona's Wastewater Operations Division operates the sanitary sewer, about 317 miles of gravity line plus 1.4 miles of force main. Flows go to the Pomona Water Reclamation Plant, overseen by the LA County Sanitation Districts, which maintain four pump stations. If several neighbors back up at once, report it to the city. If only your home does, suspect your own lateral first.

Permits and inspections come from the Building Safety Division in Development Services. Ask them what a rebuild needs, and check any contractor on the CSLB site, because California requires a licence at $1,000 or when a permit applies. See water damage repair for how rebuilding follows drying.

Insurance and flood risk for Pomona homes

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by homeowners insurance, while long-term leaks often are not. Flood is usually excluded. Check your policy, call your insurer promptly and keep every receipt.
