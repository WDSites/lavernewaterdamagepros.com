When floodwater is already inside your home

Floodwater carries soil, bacteria and debris, so treat it as unsafe from the first minute. Flood damage restoration in La Verne, CA is the removal, drying and sanitizing work that follows storm water, groundwater or a sewer backup. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, so call as soon as you can.

While you wait, stop what you can. Shut the main water valve if a pipe is involved, and cut power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel while dry. Raise valuables, keep children and pets out of the water, and wear boots and gloves if you must enter. Do not run a household vacuum on wet floors.

Why is my floor drain gurgling and sewage coming up in the shower?

Gurgling drains, toilets that bubble when a sink runs, and wastewater rising in a tub or floor drain point to a blocked main line. That is a sewage backup, and it is Category 3 water. The section on Sewage Backup Cleanup below explains what crews do and what to keep out of.

Stay out if electrical or sewage risk is present

Do not wade into water that touches outlets, appliances, cords or a panel. If you smell gas or see sparking, leave and call 911 or your utility before calling us. Stay out of sewage water entirely, and keep people and pets away until trained crews have removed it.

Which water category is it?

The category decides what can be dried and what must go.

CategoryTypical sourceHow crews treat it
Category 1Broken supply line, tub overflowExtract and dry, but clean water can worsen if left standing
Category 2Washing machine discharge, some rain intrusionRemove carpet pad, clean and disinfect before drying
Category 3Sewage, storm flooding, rising groundwaterRemove porous materials, then disinfect and dry

Standing water that sits for a day or more can move up a category.

Basement Flooding Cleanup

Many Southern California homes sit on slabs or raised foundations with no basement at all. If yours does, the same cleanup applies to below-grade rooms, garages, crawl spaces and any ground-floor room that took in storm water.

Crews pump the water down in stages, because removing it too fast can stress walls and slabs when the soil outside is still saturated. Wet carpet and pad are cut out, drywall is removed above the wet line, and affected surfaces are cleaned and disinfected. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry what remains.

In a crawl space, wet insulation and a damp vapor barrier hold moisture against framing, so crews check both. In a garage, stored cardboard and fabric should be moved to dry ground. Ask your city building department before rebuilding anything structural. Check the FEMA flood map for your address to see whether your home sits in a mapped flood zone.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Sewage is Category 3 water and a health hazard, so this is not a do-it-yourself cleanup. Crews wear protective gear, extract the liquid, and remove porous materials such as carpet, pad, drywall and insulation that the water touched. Keep the area closed off until they arrive.

Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected, then dried with air movers and dehumidifiers and checked with moisture meters. Deodorizing comes last, because odor usually means contamination is still present. Clothing, toys and other porous belongings that touched the water are often discarded, so photograph and list them first.

The blockage itself needs a licensed plumber. If the backup affects more than your home, call the city sewer or public works office to ask whether the main line is involved. Photograph the damage before cleanup, and ask your insurer about sewer backup coverage, since many policies need a separate endorsement for it. Health questions belong with your physician.

How a flood job is run

  1. 1

    Make it safe

    The crew checks for electrical hazards, shuts off what is unsafe and sorts the water by category.

  2. 2

    Extract and remove

    Pumps and extraction units pull the water, and contaminated porous materials are removed and bagged.

  3. 3

    Clean and disinfect

    Hard surfaces, framing and floors are cleaned and treated before any drying equipment is set.

  4. 4

    Dry and verify

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run until moisture meter readings return to normal.
