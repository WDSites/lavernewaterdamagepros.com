Which service do you need?

Most water losses move through the same order. Water is stopped and removed, the structure is dried, damaged material is cleaned or removed, and then repairs are made. Mold is a separate branch that starts when material stays wet.

The cards below follow that order of events, from the first hours to the rebuild. If you are not sure where you fit, call the 24/7 line and a coordinator will point you to the right step.

All services

  • Water Damage Repair

    Drywall, ceilings, insulation, flooring and paint rebuilt after the structure has been dried and tested.

  • Emergency Water Removal

    Pumps and extraction units for standing water, burst pipes and sudden leaks, with dispatch day or night.

  • Flood Damage Restoration

    Storm water, groundwater and sewage backups cleaned out, dried and sanitized by licensed, insured crews.

  • Water Mitigation

    Stopping water damage in the first 24 to 72 hours with extraction, moisture mapping and structural drying.

  • Water Damage Cleanup

    Removing soaked debris, sanitizing surfaces, deodorizing and sorting contents after water damage.

  • Mold Remediation

    Containment, removal, moisture correction and independent clearance testing for mold in La Verne homes.

How the services fit together

Water mitigation and drying come before repair, because rebuilding over wet framing traps moisture. The water damage repair page explains what is rebuilt once readings are dry.

Mold work follows moisture problems. If you already see mold, start with mold remediation, and fix the leak that fed it first.
