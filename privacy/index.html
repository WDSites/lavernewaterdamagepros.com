Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026.

Call (626) 879-0276

Information we receive

This website does not have forms, accounts or a comment system. The main way we receive information is when you call our dispatch line. During a call we may collect your phone number, your name, your address and details about the water or mold problem so a crew can be arranged.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use it

We use call information to understand your situation, arrange service and improve how the line is run. We do not use it for anything unrelated to your request.

We share the details of your request with the restoration crew assigned to your job so they can contact you and plan the work.

Third-party services on this site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded map from Google Maps. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type under their own privacy policies.

This site does not run advertising pixels or sell visitor information.

Your choices

You can tell the coordinator you do not want a call recorded or ask what we have on file. Call (626) 879-0276 or email contact@lavernewaterdamagepros.com with questions about this policy.

Water spreading right now?

The dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day. Call, say what is wet, and a coordinator will take it from there.
