What to have ready when you call

Helpful details

  • Where the water is coming from, and whether you have been able to shut it off
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and how long it has been wet
  • What the wet materials are, such as carpet, hardwood, drywall or tile
  • Whether any water touched outlets, appliances or the electrical panel
  • Your insurer's name and policy number if you plan to file a claim
  • Photos of the damage, taken before anything is moved

What happens after you call

A coordinator answers, asks whether anyone is in danger and where the water is coming from, and talks you through stopping it. Then they gather the details a crew needs and explain how scope and price will be set before any work starts.

If a crew is available, it is sent to your address. Arrival depends on the crew's schedule and conditions on the road, so the coordinator will tell you what to expect in your case. Until then, the steps on our emergency water removal page can limit the damage.

Safety first

If water is near the electrical panel or outlets, or if you smell gas, stay out and call the utility or 911 before calling us.
