Who supplies your water in Covina

Covina does not have one water company. The City of Covina Water Division serves about 33,300 people through roughly 8,500 connections, and the city also lists Golden State Water, Suburban Water Systems, Valencia Heights Water, Azusa Light and Water and Glendora Water as purveyors for parts of the area.

So your first job in a supply emergency is to read your bill. The name on it is who to call about a street leak or a meter problem, and the city's purveyor list can confirm it. Your own main valve works the same whichever company sends the water.

Before you hire anyone, ask whether the crew works to ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry's published water damage standard.

Where the leak is decides who to call

Responsibility changes at the meter and again at the property line. This is the general split, and your provider and city can confirm the details.

When water is spreading in Covina

Water moves faster than most people expect. In an hour it can run under baseboards and into the wall cavity. Close the main valve, then call our dispatch line, which is open around the clock every day.

A crew brings extraction units, air movers, LGR dehumidifiers, a moisture meter and a thermal camera. We start with the wettest, most absorbent materials, since carpet pad and drywall are hardest to save. See emergency water removal for what the pumping stage involves.

If water is still spreading

Keep it simple and stay safe. Do the steps you can reach.

  • Main valve firstClose it all the way, then open a faucet at the lowest point to drain what is left in the lines.
  • Power only if safeSwitch off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is dry. If water reaches outlets or the panel, call 911 or the utility and stay out.
  • Clear the floorLift boxes, rugs, shoes and electronics. Take furniture cushions off wet sofas and prop them to dry.
  • Protect people and petsKeep everyone out of water that came from a drain, toilet or outside. Gloves and boots are the minimum if you must enter.
  • Call the 24/7 lineGive the address, source and affected rooms. If you are not sure where the water is coming from, say so.

Sewer laterals and permits in Covina

Per the city, the Public Works sewer division maintains the local sewer system and homeowners own the house lateral. That means a backup that reaches only your fixtures is usually your pipe, while one that hits the street needs a report to the city.

Permits go through the City of Covina Building and Safety Division. One catch: some Covina-mailing addresses sit in unincorporated LA County and use the county for permits, per the city's building page. Confirm which office covers your address before you rebuild. California also requires a contractor licence once a job hits $1,000 or needs a permit, so check water damage repair contractors on the CSLB site.
