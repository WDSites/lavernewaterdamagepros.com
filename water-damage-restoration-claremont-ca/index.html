Clogged gutters and street flooding in Claremont

The city's stormwater page says leaves and clippings pushed into gutters clog catch basins and can raise the risk of street flooding. The same goes for your own roof gutters and yard drains. When they clog, water goes over the edge and down the wall or pools against the foundation.

Claremont also runs a Downspout Disconnect program and links guides on rain barrels, dry wells and bioswales. Those can help, but redirected roof water has to land away from the house. Ask the city before you reroute a downspout, and check that the new path slopes away from your walls.

Where roof and yard water goes wrong

Most storm-related interior damage starts outside. Here is where we see the path break down and what a homeowner can check.

Water, sewer and permits in Claremont

Claremont states that it does not own or run the water service. Golden State Water Company does, and Three Valleys Municipal Water District, based in Claremont, is a wholesale supplier. For no-water or street-meter problems, call the provider and not the city.

The city's sewer page says main lines are maintained by its Community Services Department and property owners maintain their laterals. That split matters in a backup: if the whole street is slow, tell the city, and if only your fixtures back up, the lateral is probably yours.

Permits go through the Building Division at 207 Harvard Avenue. California requires a contractor licence once labor and materials reach $1,000 or a permit is needed. Use the CSLB site to verify any contractor, including for water damage repair.

Flood risk and what to look up for your address

The city has a Local Hazard Mitigation Plan approved by FEMA, and you can ask the city for it. We cannot tell you your flood zone from here, so check the FEMA flood map for your address.

Flood is usually excluded from standard homeowners policies, so a separate flood policy is a conversation for your insurer. If water has already come in, read our mold remediation page before you tear out anything that may be moldy.
